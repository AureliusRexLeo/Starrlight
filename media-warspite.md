# Warspite (Media PC)
Last updated: 2026-11-02

## Why
The living-room theater box: playback and disc ripping only. Direct-play 4K HDR10 with Atmos bitstreamed to the AV receiver. Light emulation and Steam Remote Play. Jellyfin client only; transcoding happens elsewhere.

## Target (planned build)
- CPU: Upgrade TBD, chosen later for heavier local emulation.
- Board: AM5.
- GPU: GTX 1080. NVIDIA handles HDR10 and Atmos bitstreaming well on Linux.
- RAM: 16gb DDR5, upgraded along with the CPU.
- Storage: 1tb nmve (reliabilty and durability highest consideration).
- Optical: external USB Blu-ray burner.
- OS: Linux with Kodi/mpv.

## Starting build
A friend is giving me a Ryzen 7 8700F, an AM5 board, and a cooler for free. That gets Warspite running now as a temporary media PC on a platform I can upgrade.
The 8700F stays until I pick its replacement and drop it in. It has no built-in graphics, so the GTX 1080 is required.
